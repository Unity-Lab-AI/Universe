# Against the field

> The honest comparison: Universe beside every well-known program that makes or shows a universe, and the two tiny pioneers that showed the way. A tick means it is there; a dash means it is not; a few words say how far. Each project's own published numbers and pages, linked where a claim is checked.

## What each needs, and how Universe runs

| | Needs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Universe** | Windows 10 or 11, any Direct3D 12 card (on Linux through Wine, with the script in the zip). No install size to speak of (1.8 MB), no download beyond the 0.80 MB installer | Every pixel is worked out on the card from equations, so the card does the work; a card with little memory starts at a lower render scale by itself, and **View > GPU use** holds the card at the share you choose |
| [SpaceEngine](https://store.steampowered.com/app/314650/SpaceEngine/ "SpaceEngine on Steam") | GTX 1050 Ti or RX 460 with 3 GB of card memory, 6 GB of RAM, 4 GB of disk (recommended: GTX 1060, 8 GB, 50 GB) | The nearest in scope: galaxies to a world's ground, with textures for the real worlds |
| [Universe Sandbox](https://universesandbox.com/faq/ "Universe Sandbox FAQ") | A Direct3D 12 card with 4 GB of card memory, 4 GB of RAM, 4 GB of disk | Simulates a few hundred bodies at once, not a universe |
| [Elite Dangerous](https://www.pcgamesn.com/elite-dangerous/system-requirements "Elite Dangerous system requirements, PCGamesN") | GTX 470 or R7 240, 6 GB of RAM, 25 GB of disk; Odyssey: GTX 780 with 3 GB, 8 GB of RAM, 75 GB | A full game with ships, trade, missions and sound |
| [No Man's Sky](https://www.pcgamesn.com/no-mans-sky/system-requirements "No Man's Sky system requirements, PCGamesN") | GTX 1060 with 3 GB, RX 470, or Intel UHD 630 graphics; 8 GB of RAM; 15 GB of disk | A full game, with art and sound, that now runs on built-in graphics |
| [Celestia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestia "Celestia on Wikipedia") 1.6 | Any computer of the last twenty years | Catalogues drawn as they are |
| [Pioneer](https://wiki.pioneerspacesim.net/wiki/FAQ "Pioneer FAQ") | An OpenGL 3.2 card, about 550 MB of disk | Open source, Windows and Linux |

## Where it stands

Nothing in these tables goes from the cosmic web to a quark in one unbroken camera, and nothing but the two tiny pioneers comes near the size. Noctis is the closest in spirit: one person, one galaxy, a few megabytes, twenty years ago. SpaceEngine is the closest in scope, more than two thousand times the size, and it stops at the ground.

## To be fair

The others carry what Universe doesn't yet: sound, stored art and textures, gameplay, ships, trade, missions, years of polish. Universe is a 0.1 preview with no life on its worlds yet. Its size is small because every pixel is worked out on the graphics card from equations, so it needs a Direct3D 12 card and does its work there; the frame rate at the grains, where the ground under the camera is made finest, is the price, and the GPU-use setting is the answer.
