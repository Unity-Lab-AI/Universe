Universe 0.1.0
Made by the Unity AI Lab team - https://unityailab.com

One universe you can zoom through without a break: from the cosmic web of galaxies, into a galaxy and its stars, a star's
worlds, down to a planet's ground, through its grains and crystals, into an atom, its nucleus, a proton and its quarks.
Every part of it comes from one seed: the same seed always makes the same universe.

Start
  The landing page opens first. Type a seed (or press Random, or leave it blank for a new one), then Begin, or Take the
  tour for a five-minute film of the whole journey (tick Film the tour to save it as an MP4 clip). Clips lists the
  clips filmed so far: Play one, or Open folder to see where they are. Settings and the Guide and wiki are there too.

Controls
  Click                   pick a galaxy, star, planet, grain or atom: the camera turns to it and the wheel flies to it
  Mouse wheel             fly in or out toward what the mouse points at
  Ctrl + mouse wheel      eight times as fast
  W A S D, Q / E          fly; Shift for four times as fast
  Left-drag               circle round what you picked, or look around
  Right click             let go of what you picked
  T                       the tour on or off
  F12                     a screenshot (PNG)          F9   start or stop a clip (MP4)       both in the captures folder
  H                       the scale on screen         Space  pause time      , and .  slower and faster
  Esc                     close

Needs: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a graphics card that runs Direct3D 12. It runs in a window and never changes your
display settings.

Uninstall: from Windows' Apps list, or "Uninstall Universe.exe" in the install folder.

The source, and everything to build it yourself: https://git.unityailab.com/GFourteen/Universe
