===============================================================================
   U N I V E R S E                                                      0.1.0
   One camera. One universe. From the cosmic web down to a quark.
   Made by the Unity AI Lab  -  https://unityailab.com
===============================================================================

  Zoom through one universe without a break: the cosmic web of galaxies, a
  galaxy and its stars, a star's worlds, a world's ground, its grains and
  crystals, an atom, its nucleus, a proton and its quarks.

  Everything grows from one seed. The same seed always makes the same universe.

  And it's a toolkit: every kind of galaxy, star and world is a template in
  plain text in the content folder. Change them, add your own, and build
  universes for your own games and simulations.

  Built from nothing: no engine, no libraries, no art, 1.7 MB installed,
  MIT licensed. Take it for any project that needs a universe.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  START
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The landing page opens first.

    Seed ............ type one, press Random, or leave it blank for a new one
    Begin ........... fly it yourself
    Take the tour ... a film of the whole journey
    Film the tour ... tick it to save the tour as an MP4 clip
    Clips ........... play what you've filmed, or Open folder to find them

  Start page, on the menu bar, brings the landing page back.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONTROLS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Mouse wheel ......... fly toward what the mouse points at
    Ctrl + wheel ........ eight times as fast
    W A S D, Q / E ...... fly; hold Shift for four times as fast
    Left click .......... pick a galaxy, star, world, grain or atom
    Right click ......... let go of what you picked
    Left drag ........... circle round what you picked, or look around
    Space ............... pause time         , and .   slower and faster
    H ................... the scale bar and info cards, on or off
    T ................... the tour; carries a paused tour on
    R ................... starts a paused tour again
    F12 ................. a screenshot (PNG)
    F9 .................. start or stop a clip (MP4)
    Esc ................. close

  A click, the wheel or a key pauses the tour, and a note says why.
  Help, on the menu bar, has every document, beautifully laid out;
  Documents: what's here lists them all and what each is for.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GOOD TO KNOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Needs ......... Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a Direct3D 12 graphics card
    Display ....... runs in a window and never changes your display settings
    Captures ...... screenshots and clips go in the captures folder
    Stats ......... frames a second with GPU and CPU stats on screen: tick
                    boxes on the landing page, or the View menu
    Updating ...... installing over it offers to delete old clips, screenshots
                    and settings, each on its own
    Uninstall ..... Windows' Apps list, or "Uninstall Universe.exe" here
    Build your own  https://git.unityailab.com/GFourteen/Universe
