> **One camera, one universe.** A visible universe 13.4 billion light-years across, about 100 billion galaxies, down to the quarks inside a grain of sand, with no loading screens along the way.

## What it is

A whole universe you can fly through, in one unbroken zoom:

| Scale | What you find there |
|---|---|
| The cosmic web | About 100 billion galaxies along filaments and walls, round vast thin voids |
| A galaxy | About a hundred billion stars, made round you as you fly among them |
| A star | Its worlds, moons, belts, comets and far icy cloud |
| A world | Its ground, seas, clouds and air, alive on its own clock |
| The ground | Stones, grains, the crystal of their mineral |
| The matter | Atoms, their electrons, the nucleus, protons, neutrons and quarks |

## Made, not stored

Everything is worked out live from one number, the **seed**, by our own code. There are no photos, no painted backdrops and no outside code. The same seed always gives the same universe.

## A dead universe, still alive

There's no life here yet, but the worlds aren't frozen. Their insides cool, plates drift, volcanoes erupt, ice ages come and go, and rocks from space leave craters.
