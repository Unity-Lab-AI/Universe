The canonical forms

★ The combiner equation (the master of masters)

Every form below is a transform over one shared variable space — the wavelet coefficient field. This is the “combiner”: the image, the sound and the fractal are all functions of the SAME \(C\), which is exactly what makes them equatable between master equations (and what lets the reverse features run):

$$ C = \{\, (r_k,\, c_k,\, v_k) : k = 1\ldots N \,\}\qquad(\text{+ palette } P,\ \text{dims } W\times H) $$

each kept term \(k\) has row \(r_k\), column/band \(c_k\) (its \(\psi\) position) and value \(v_k\). The three master maps:

$$ \Phi_{\text{img}}(C) = \sum_k v_k\,\psi_{r_k,c_k}(x,y),\ \ \text{RGB}\leftarrow\text{YCbCr} \qquad(\textbf{form 0}) $$ $$ \Phi_{\text{snd}}(C) = \Big(\ \ell_k=\lfloor\log_2\max(r_k,c_k,1)\rfloor,\ \ f_k=f_{\text{lo}}\big(\tfrac{f_{\text{hi}}}{f_{\text{lo}}}\big)^{\ell_k/\ell_{\max}},\ \ a_k=\tfrac{|v_k|}{\max|v|},\ \ t_k=\tfrac{\text{rank}_k}{N}T\ \Big)\quad(\textbf{form 9}) $$ $$ \Phi_{\text{frc}}(C) = \{A_k,\mathbf{t}_k,w_k\}\ \text{fitted to } \Phi_{\text{img}}(C);\qquad \text{growth} = \text{iterate } x^3 - 2y^3 = 3\ \ (\text{Newton}) \quad(\textbf{form 7}) $$

Original vs equation — both faces of the same file. The original you put in (the source picture \(I_0\) or the source audio) is the ground truth; \(\Phi_{\text{img}}(C)\) and \(\Phi_{\text{snd}}(C)\) are its equational faces re-synthesised from the field \(C\). A file made on the site carries the original alongside \(C\), so the Combiner, Your equations and the Viewer let you toggle image: original / equation and sound: original / equation — the full combination pulled from any one file (an image-source has no original sound, a sound-source no original picture; the equation supplies the missing modality).

Inverses exist only where information is preserved: \(\Phi_{\text{snd}}^{-1}\) recovers \(C\) — and therefore the image via \(\Phi_{\text{img}}\) — only when each note encodes position (pitch→band, pan→column, duration→row, amplitude→\(|v_k|\)). The everyday few-notes playback discards position, so it is not invertible. The Combiner realises sound→image three ways: a .uvme/.wav carries the exact \(C\) in a data chunk (byte-exact); an equational sound encodes the compact \(C\) in the waveform itself via a 16-tone MFSK modem demodulated by Goertzel (exact to the compact field, no chunk); and the additive loader derives a \(C\) from any plain sound's spectrum (generated, not exact). A byte-exact dense field is megabytes — impossible as a listenable waveform — so the equational sound carries the compact field and the dense exact image stays on the chunked .uvme. The forms below instantiate these maps in full.

0 · Image reconstruction (primary)

Each image is rebuilt exactly — to a deviation tolerance — as a sum of CDF 9/7 biorthogonal wavelet basis-function terms per YCbCr channel (the JPEG2000 wavelet). A per-image count of coefficients \(c_k\) (roughly 130k for a sparse piece up to ~1.5M for a dense one), each paired with a wavelet \(\psi\) at a scale and position, sum back to the real pattern, colours, and lines:

$$ \text{channel}(x,y) = \sum_{k} c_k\,\psi_{j_k}(x - x_k,\; y - y_k) $$

\(\psi\) is the CDF 9/7 biorthogonal wavelet, applied separably (rows then columns) over multiple dyadic levels via the lifting scheme — predict/update passes with constants \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta\) and a scaling \(K\). Unlike the Haar box function it has no finite closed form, which is exactly why it captures smooth gradients and fine spark detail at native resolution without blocky artifacts.

$$ R = Y + 1.402(C_r-\tfrac12),\;\; G = Y - 0.344(C_b-\tfrac12) - 0.714(C_r-\tfrac12),\;\; B = Y + 1.772(C_b-\tfrac12) $$

Dropping the weakest terms increases deviation (more abstract); keeping all of them reproduces the image to within the tolerance. The per-image term lists are searchable below and rendered live on each viewer.

This same forward transform runs in your browser: on Your equations you can upload any image and it is turned into its own \(c_k\) coefficients client-side (nothing uploaded), saved locally to replay — the analyzer is a machine-exact inverse-pair of the decoder above.

1 · Line-vector field

Each image is sharpened with an unsharp mask, Canny-edged, and reduced to line segments with a probabilistic Hough transform. Near-colinear "same-trajectory" segments are decimated to a single representative:

$$ V = \{\, (\mathbf{p}_0,\mathbf{p}_1,\theta,\ell) \,\},\qquad \theta = \operatorname{atan2}(\Delta y,\Delta x)\bmod \pi,\quad \ell = \lVert \mathbf{p}_1-\mathbf{p}_0\rVert $$

A structure tensor gives a coarse dominant-orientation field \( \theta(i,j)=\tfrac12\operatorname{atan2}(2J_{xy},\,J_{xx}-J_{yy}) \) with coherence \( \sqrt{(J_{xx}-J_{yy})^2+4J_{xy}^2}/(J_{xx}+J_{yy}) \).

2 · Colour distribution

Each RGB channel's histogram is fitted as a 3-component Gaussian mixture (EM), and the dominant palette is extracted by k-means:

$$ p_c(x) = \sum_{k=1}^{3} w_k\,\mathcal{N}(x;\mu_k,\sigma_k^2),\qquad C(\text{image}) \approx \{\, w_i\cdot(r_i,g_i,b_i) \,\} $$

3 · Luminance field & density aggregation

Rec.709 luminance, aggregated into a density grid (a fractal-flame "density" analogue):

$$ L(x,y) = 0.2126\,R + 0.7152\,G + 0.0722\,B,\qquad D(i,j) = \sum_{(x,y)\in \text{cell}(i,j)} L(x,y) $$

4 · Lumen-degradation propagation

A decimated gradient field describing how luminance falls off across the frame:

$$ \nabla L(i,j) = \left(\tfrac{\partial L}{\partial x},\,\tfrac{\partial L}{\partial y}\right), \qquad \text{magnitude } \lVert\nabla L\rVert \text{ on a coarse grid} $$

5 · Morph-allotment gradients

Normalized gradient maps reserved for morphing between templates:

$$ M(i,j) = \frac{\nabla L(i,j)}{\max_{i,j}\lVert\nabla L\rVert} $$

6 · Waveform stereotype

The radially-averaged 2-D Fourier power spectrum is the template's waveform fingerprint; its log-log slope \(\beta\) is the spectral / fractal falloff exponent:

$$ P(f) \;\propto\; f^{-\beta},\qquad \beta = -\,\frac{d\,\log P}{d\,\log f} $$

7 · Generative IFS (regeneration)

An approximate iterated function system whose affine maps regenerate a fractalized image in the source's family. A point is iterated, choosing map \(k\) with probability \(w_k\):

$$ \mathbf{x}_{n+1} = A_k\,\mathbf{x}_n + \mathbf{t}_k,\qquad A_k=\begin{bmatrix} a&b\\ c&d \end{bmatrix},\; \mathbf{t}_k=\begin{bmatrix} e\\ f \end{bmatrix},\quad \Pr(k)=w_k $$

The maps are fitted by a self-similarity collage search over scale×rotation; this is an approximation, not an exact flame inversion. Separately, the ∞ zoom-fractalize on the viewer (and the world engine, which holds its morph while you zoom) grows new detail where a zoomed image runs out: it solves the cubic \(x^3-2y^3=K\) (and its 3D form \(x^3-2y^3-z^3=K\)) plus a complex Newton field \(z^3-K=0\), warped by sine/cosine navigators across all axes, with fBm micro-detail whose frequency rides the zoom — so it refines forever, never bottoming out, coloured from the piece's palette. The home flight portal runs this exact chain live: Unity Born is equationalized into a single equation plane in a 3D frame and you dive into its depth (the equation fractalizes) by scrolling, tilting with drag / WASD·QE, R to home — the same \(\Phi_{\text{frc}}\) growth, piloted.

8 · Box-counting fractal dimension

The Minkowski–Bouligand dimension of the edge set, from box counts \(N(\varepsilon)\) at scales \(\varepsilon\):

$$ D = \lim_{\varepsilon\to 0} \frac{\log N(\varepsilon)}{\log(1/\varepsilon)} $$

9 · The master music equation (equation → sound)

The sound is not a tune added on top — there is no scale, key, melody or rhythm pattern anywhere. Every note is a pure function of the image's own luma wavelet coefficients \(c_k\) at grid position \((r_k,c_k)\). Documented here so anyone can recreate it from a template alone:

Each coefficient's wavelet band (its dyadic scale level) sets the octave — coarse bands play bass, fine bands play treble — giving a wide register straight from the maths:

$$ \ell_k=\big\lfloor\log_2\max(r_k,c_k,1)\big\rfloor,\qquad f_k=f_{\text{lo}}\left(\tfrac{f_{\text{hi}}}{f_{\text{lo}}}\right)^{\ell_k/\ell_{\max}} \!\!\left(1+\tfrac{1}{16}\,u_k\right) $$

with \(f_{\text{lo}}=40\,\text{Hz}\), \(f_{\text{hi}}=3200\,\text{Hz}\), \(\ell_{\max}=\lfloor\log_2\max(W,H)\rfloor\), and \(u_k\in[0,1)\) the fractional position inside the band. Loudness is the coefficient magnitude. The strongest few coefficients per band are chosen so every octave speaks, then read out in a stride-rotated round-robin across the bands — each successive note jumps about half the register and the starting band drifts each round, so the melody leaps across octaves instead of climbing a low→high scale (that ascending sweep was an audible strobe). \(\text{rank}_k\) is a note's position in that read-out order:

$$ a_k=\frac{|c_k|}{\max_j|c_j|},\qquad t_k=\frac{\text{rank}_k}{N}\,T $$

Stereo pan comes from the dominant palette hue; a continuous scheduler crossfades one piece's note-set into the next by the live morph amount, so in the world engine the music morphs and merges with the images without pauses. No notes are stored or pre-composed — feed any template's \(c_k\) through these formulas and you get the same music.

Reject ladder (what never becomes an equation)

  • Fog / blur — low Laplacian variance + low edge density + low high-frequency energy ("no line vectors").
  • TV-snow static — high entropy + flat spectrum + low lag-1 autocorrelation ("high noise volume").
  • Text / emoji-dominant — uniform glyph rows filling the frame, vetoed when strong line-vector structure is present (a small title caption is ignored).
  • Non-unique — equation signature too close (cosine) to an already-kept template.
