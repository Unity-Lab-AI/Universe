Thesis — one equation for image, sound & motion

Uni Vs Matics rests on a single claim: a picture, a sound and a moving clip are not three kinds of file — they are three views of one shared mathematical object, a sparse CDF 9/7 wavelet coefficient field C. Below is that thesis stated plainly, and — because a claim that can't fail isn't science — a Popperian falsification log: every hypothesis we actually tried to break, what broke, and what we shipped in its place. No smoke. Where a thing didn't work, it says so.

The thesis (the positive claim)

One shared coefficient field C = {(r_k, c_k, v_k)} — rows, wavelet bands and values — is the variable space. Every modality is a deterministic transform of the same C:

Φimg(C) = Σk vk·ψ(rk,ck)  ·  Φsnd(C) → (ℓk,fk,ak,tk)  ·  Φfrc(C) → IFS  ·  V = { Ct } (motion)
  • Resolution-independent. An equation has no pixel grid. The picture is solved at whatever resolution you ask for; past the source's analyzed detail the same equation keeps generating self-similar structure.
  • Modality-independent. The same field is the picture (Φimg), the music (Φsnd), the fractal (Φfrc), and — per frame — a video. Convert in any direction through C.
  • One artifact, self-describing. The 🧠 .uvme is a real WAV that plays as audio and carries the exact field in a standard chunk — a Rosetta Stone a single extract() reads out as image / png / wav / sound / fractal.
  • Classical, not AI. CDF 9/7 wavelets + signal processing — deterministic, inspectable, offline, private. No trained model, no inference, no cloud.

Falsification log — what we tried to break, and what broke

Each entry is a hypothesis we held, the test we ran, and the verdict. A REJECTED badge means the idea failed and was dropped; the line after it is what we shipped instead.

H1 — "A single file can be a valid .wav AND .png AND .jpg at once."falsified

Every one of those formats demands its own magic bytes at byte 0; they collide. A container is one format at a time. Shipped instead: the polyglot is the equation + master code, not the wrapper — the .uvme Rosetta Stone derives any representation from C on demand.

H2 — "The equational sound can carry the full dense field at a usable size."falsified

Encoding hundreds of thousands of coefficients as audible MFSK tones produces enormous files. Measured: the exact field as a data chunk is ~131× smaller than the same field rendered as listenable sound. Shipped instead: the exact field rides the data chunk; the equational sound carries a compact field (still byte-exact to what it encodes, invertible) — exact belongs in data, not in tone.

H3 — "A 3D fly-through is the best way to present the field."rejected

The "Innerspace" fly-through read as a warpspeed/starfield mess — it dramatized motion without showing the maths. Shipped instead: a flat layered compositor (the world engine) + an ∞ zoom-fractalize on a single still, which actually exposes the equation's structure.

H4 — "A colour-grade continuation stage improves the deep zoom" (the #271 three-stage model).falsified

The idea: past native detail, continue the silhouette-edge's two-tone colour gradient across the viewport (vector-aligned) before the fractal takes over. In testing the flat viewport-filling gradient was an opaque wash that disassociated fine detail — worse than doing nothing. Shipped instead: the fractal propagates out of the image's own edges and vectors (Sobel-masked + additively composited), growing along the real structure rather than pasted over it.

H5 — "Zooming in reveals infinite real detail."falsified

Past a source's analyzed resolution there is no finer real detail — it isn't hidden in the file. Stated honestly instead: the fractal is an honest infinite fill, the equation's own self-similar continuation in the picture's colours — not recovered reality. Raising the analysis resolution (the scanner depth) is what deepens the real fabric before the fill begins.

H6 — "This is novel / unprecedented."not claimed

A light online cross-check (not a peer review) found the building blocks are established prior art — shared-wavelet multimodal representations, RIFF chunk side-data, audio-modem/SSTV-style sound encodings. We make no firm novelty claim; only the specific combination (one field → exact image + invertible sound + image-driven fractal via one extract) is, to our knowledge, uncommon — unconfirmed. If you published it first with peer-reviewed material, email contact@unityailab.com and we'll reference it.

What survived (the corroborated core)

The .uvme round-trips byte-for-byte.held

Verified in a real browser: image → C → .uvme → C → image is 0.00% loss on every test image; a found sound converted in and round-tripped at PSNR ∞. See Proven.

Cross-modal conversion works in every direction.held

sound ↔ image ↔ gif ↔ mp4, plus the equational answers (image, original sound, music-of-the-equation, equational images) — all derived from one field. Headless-verified across every portal.

It runs offline, on classical maths, with no AI.held

Everything is solved client-side from the coefficients — no upload, no model, no inference. The only hard requirement is a desktop GPU for the live engines (a phone can read everything but can't render them).

Method

We hold a "code it right the first time" discipline backed by manual + headless verification rather than a test suite: each claim above was exercised in a real headless browser (the round-trips, the cross-conversions, the portal behaviours) and then signed off live on a real GPU before shipping. Negative results — H1–H6 — are kept on the record here precisely because they shaped what got built. The thesis is only as strong as the hypotheses it survived.

Honest scope: this is an engineering thesis, not a peer-reviewed paper. The maths is classical and credited (Equations, provenance); the artwork is a real corpus by a schizophrenic artist with photographic memory. Prior-art corrections welcome at contact@unityailab.com.
